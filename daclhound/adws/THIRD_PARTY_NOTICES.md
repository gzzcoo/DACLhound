The protocol implementation in `_protocol/` is vendored from
[adwsdomaindump](https://github.com/mverschu/adwsdomaindump), which adapts
Soaphound's ADWS implementation. It is distributed under the MIT license;
the license text and copyright notices are in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`.
DACLhound's collector, LDAP query orchestration, descriptor parser, and
schema GUID resolution are maintained separately in this package.
